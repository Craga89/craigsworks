how i like to work

A few things I hold to

Habits that have served me well, and that you'd see in anything we do together.

  • Measure first, then change

    Every saving and speed-up I've made started with data, and landed with the evidence alongside it.

  • One reviewed change, not a long-lived branch

    Big migrations scripted and landed in one go, so the codebase never splits into "old" and "new".

  • Close doors rather than add locks

    Remove whole classes of risk, like public servers or standing keys, instead of piling on controls.

  • Plain English for the people paying

    Technical work explained in terms of outcomes, from engineers to the board.

  • Fix the system, not the person

    Incidents end in better tooling and runbooks, never finger-pointing.

  • Remove friction before adding process

    Most teams need fewer hoops, not more. Process should change when the team does.
