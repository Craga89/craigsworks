This website

The site sets no cookies, runs no analytics or advertising, and loads nothing from other companies: its fonts and scripts are served from craigsworks.com itself. The one thing it remembers is a "pause animations" choice, kept in your own browser's storage and never sent anywhere.

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. Like any web host, GitHub may keep technical logs of visits (such as IP addresses) to run and secure its service; see GitHub's privacy statement.

When you get in touch

If you email or book a call, I'll use your name, contact details and what you tell me to reply, arrange the call and, if we work together, to deliver and invoice the work. The lawful bases are taking steps towards a contract and my legitimate interest in answering enquiries. I keep enquiries that don't go anywhere for up to 12 months, and business records for as long as the law requires (six years for tax).

I don't sell your information or use it for marketing, and I only share it where the law requires.
