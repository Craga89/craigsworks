what you get

A plan you keep, with or without me

The output is written for the people who'll act on it, and for the people who'll fund it.

  • A ranked plan

    Every finding with its impact, rough effort and the reasoning, in the order I'd tackle them.

  • A plain-English summary

    One page for founders and leadership: where things stand and what it would take to move.

  • A walkthrough with the team

questions

About the health check

Do you need access to production?

Usually not. Read-only access to the code, the CI set-up and the cloud configuration covers most of it, and anything sensitive can be walked through on a call instead.

Can we act on the plan without you?

Yes, that's the point. It's written so your team can pick it up, and it's yours to keep either way.

Can it help with due diligence or a big customer's security review?

It can be a good starting point. I've been through an acquisition's due diligence and plenty of security questionnaires, so I know the kinds of questions that come up.

Not sure where to start?

That's what the health check is for. Tell me a bit about your team on a free call and I'll suggest a scope that fits.
